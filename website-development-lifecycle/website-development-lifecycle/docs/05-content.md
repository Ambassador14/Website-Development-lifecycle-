# 5. Content Preparation

Prepare content before or alongside development.

Content may include:
- Website copy
- Images
- Videos
- Product information
- Blog posts
- Icons
- Documents
- FAQs
- SEO metadata

Content should be clear, accurate, accessible, relevant, and easy to scan.

Do not treat content as an afterthought. Content affects design, layout, SEO, accessibility, and user experience.
